## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup hot water
- 3 tsp ginger
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- < 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 Tbsp sweetener (optional)
- hot sauce to taste


## Instructions
1. Measure out peanut butter into glass jar
2. Add hot water
3. Add other ingredients and mix together
4. Serve
