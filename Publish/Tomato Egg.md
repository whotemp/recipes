## Ingredients
- 2 tsp ginger
- 3 tomatoes (450g, or 1 can)
- 3  eggs with some salt
- 1 tbsp cornstarch (for slurry)
- 4 tbsp water (for slurry)
- 2 tbsp oil
- 3 tbsp water
- 2 tbsp ketchup

## Instructions
1. Roughly dice tomatoes
2. Beat eggs together with salt
3. Prepare slurry, mix the water into cornstarch
4. Cook eggs until 80% cooked, remove eggs into beating bowl
5. Stir fry ginger briefly, add tomatoes, add a little water and cook tomatoes down
6. Add in cornstarch, slurry, and ketchup
7. Add in eggs
8. Serve
